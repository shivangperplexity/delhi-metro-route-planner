    How the route was computed

    Stations are graph vertices, track between neighbouring stations are weighted edges, and changing lines is a transfer edge.

    Find a route to compare algorithms on it.

    Dijkstra

    Greedy: always settles the closest unsettled station using a binary-heap priority queue. Gives the fastest or shortest route.

    O((V + E) log V)

    A* search

    Dijkstra plus a straight-line (haversine) estimate to the destination, so it explores toward the goal and settles fewer nodes.

    O((V + E) log V)

    0-1 BFS

    Riding to the next station costs 1, changing line costs 0. A double-ended queue finds the fewest-stop route without a heap.

    O(V + E)

    Travel-time map

    Single-source Dijkstra from the origin to every station, coloured by minutes away, like an isochrone.

    O((V + E) log V)
